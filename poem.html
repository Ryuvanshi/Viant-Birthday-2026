A little poem, just for you

For the Blue Hydrangea

Some feelings are easier to leave between the lines.

✿

If the world were only a garden,
you would not need to be its brightest flower.
You would not need to bloom for anyone,
or prove your worth with every passing hour.

Even on the days your petals fold,
when the sky forgets its blue,
the garden does not think you lesser
it simply makes a little room for you.

And somewhere in that quiet field,
beneath the rain and morning dew,
a blue hydrangea keeps its color
not because it must,
but because it is beautifully, wholly you.

Seasons change. The light moves on.
Some paths may part, some skies turn gray.
Still, I hope life brings you gentle mornings
and gives you reasons to smile along the way.

So grow at your pace, in your own time;
let no hurried hand decide your spring.
There is no single way for flowers to bloom
may you find the joy that each new season brings.

— with warmth, always. Ryu

No need to find a meaning in every line.
I just hope a little kindness stays with you.
