A little wish for you

For All the Tomorrows Ahead

Some wishes are too lovely to keep to ourselves.
Shall we send a few into the sky?

One little wish at a time

And so, the night settles

For all the tomorrows ahead.

May life bring you beautiful things,

in your own time and in your own way.

May tomorrow be a little kinder,

and may you always find a reason to smile.
